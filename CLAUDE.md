# CLAUDE.md — Green-Bond-Greenium builder instructions

Project-specific conventions and gotchas. Read before changing the pipeline.

## The one rule: lead with the twins, not the fitted curve
The **identical-twin matched-pair spread** is the headline estimate — model-free,
just `(conventional yield − green yield)`. The liquidity-adjusted FE panel is
**corroboration**, not the lead. (France was dropped — see below — precisely because
a fitted-curve estimate is the kind of over-fitted number this rule warns against.)

## Twin pairs (the identification spine)
- `data/manual/twin_pairs.csv` is **rebuilt at fetch time** by `fetch_bundesbank.py`
  from the Bundesbank workbook's own green/conventional labels — do not hand-edit it;
  edits are overwritten on the next live run. Within a pair the coupon and maturity
  are **identical by construction** (the twin is matched on exactly those), which is
  what makes the spread like-for-like. ISINs are real and Luhn-valid (e.g. the 10y
  green Bund 2030 is `DE0001030708`, twin `DE0001102507`).
- (Historical note) `isin_verified` was a flag from the earlier hand-keyed list. The verified anchor was the 10y green Bund 2030
  (`DE0001030757`, 0%, 15 Aug 2030, first issue 2 Sep 2020). **Confirm the other
  ISINs and the live Bundesbank series keys at fetch time** (plan §11.3) — IDs change
  as new green securities are listed; the fetcher logs the keys it used.

## Data mode (important)
Every artefact is stamped with `data_mode`. The **default is `live`** — the German
headline runs on real Bundesbank data and that is what is published.
- `live` — real daily yields from the Bundesbank "prices and yields of listed
  Federal securities" monthly Excel workbooks (NOT the SDMX API — per-ISIN yields
  are not on the API; see `01_fetch/fetch_bundesbank.py`). The fetcher crawls the
  monthly XLSX archive, parses one sheet per trading day, finds the green bonds by
  their "… Green" label, and matches each to its conventional twin by identical
  coupon+maturity. It rebuilds `data/manual/twin_pairs.csv` from those labels (real,
  Luhn-valid ISINs + issue volumes), so the twin set is data-derived, not hand-keyed.
  ECB SDW supplies the conventional euro-area curve.
- `demo-synthetic` — a deterministic synthetic panel generated by
  `01_fetch/make_demo_data.py` ONLY as a fallback when the live Bundesbank archive is
  unreachable. The dashboard/report show a banner. **Synthetic results are
  illustrative only — never quote them as empirical.**

## Reachability reality (corrected 2026-06)
The Bundesbank workbook archive is NOT bot-blocked here; it answers 200. The earlier
"endpoints block automated requests" assumption (carried from the FRED project) was
wrong for Germany. What *is* blocked: the **AFT** (French green-OAT) site returns 403
to automated requests, and there is no free machine-readable per-ISIN green-OAT yield
series anywhere — that, plus the fitted-curve model risk, is why **France was dropped
entirely** (fetchers, NSS-curve stage, dashboard/report panels all removed). If a
clean green-OAT yield source ever turns up, France is a re-add, not a debug.

## Yield-convention harmonisation (a central gotcha)
Both legs of a German twin come from the **same Bundesbank quote basis** (annual /
actual-actual), so `greenium = conv − green` is differenced directly. This is
asserted in `tests/test_entry_dates.py` and logged in `panel_meta.json`. **Never
difference yields from two sources without checking the quote basis first.**

## Liquidity is the confound, not an afterthought
The green leg is smaller/less liquid. The twin design fixes issuer/coupon/maturity/
tax; the **only** liquidity dimension that varies within a pair×date cell is **issue
size** (age and on/off-the-run are identical across twins on a given day). So the FE
panel adjusts the green dummy for `log_amount_out` and reports **raw vs adjusted** —
the gap is the liquidity premium. Do not claim a pure "green preference" without
showing the adjusted number.

## Inference (mandatory, because the signal is a few bp)
- **Block bootstrap** (stationary, Politis–Romano) for CIs on every level and on the
  compression trend (`pipeline/stats.py`).
- **Newey–West HAC** SE for the mean greenium; **cluster-robust** (by pair) SEs for
  the panel.
- Headline level is computed on **weekly** means; daily/monthly are robustness.
- Report with and without the COVID / 2022 stress windows.

## Refutation must run and be reported
`refutation.py` produces: (a) placebo — a conventional leg's deviation from the
conventional NSS curve should be ≈ 0 with a CI spanning zero (greenium is not a
maturity artefact); (b) liquidity-only model (greenium at equal liquidity); (c)
sign stability across pairs. A null or liquidity-only outcome is reported honestly,
not hidden.

## Determinism & artefacts
- `SEED = config.yaml:seed` (20260615), recorded in every results JSON.
- `data/raw/`, `data/interim/` are gitignored; `data/processed/`, `data/facts/`,
  `data/manual/` and `site/data/` are committed (small) so `--offline` rebuilds.
- All `site/data/*.json` must pass the browser-parseable guard (no NaN/Infinity);
  `common.write_json` enforces it and the CI re-checks.

## Dependencies
Pinned for Python 3.11. The FE panel is implemented via a within (pair×date)
transform + cluster-robust OLS in `pipeline/stats.py` (no `linearmodels`), and the
block bootstrap is hand-rolled (no `arch`) — lighter and fully reproducible. NSS is
`scipy.optimize.least_squares`.
